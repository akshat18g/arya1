Privacy Policy - Dash Line Pre-registration Reward

Last updated: July 2, 2025

Thank you for your interest in our game Dash Line. We value your privacy and are committed to providing a transparent and fair experience to all our users.

Pre-registration Reward Details

Users who pre-register for Dash Line will receive a lifetime ad-free version of the game. This offer is valid as long as the game is not uninstalled after it is first downloaded and launched post-release.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data directly for the purpose of this reward. However, standard data such as app install status may be tracked anonymously to verify eligibility for the reward.

Eligibility Requirements

  • You must pre-register for Dash Line before the official release.
  • You must install and launch the game shortly after release.
  • You must not delete the game. Uninstalling and reinstalling may void the ad-free reward status.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this reward policy or need assistance, feel free to reach out to us at contact@aryaleelastudios.com.
